# Debt Management, Emergency Funds, and Savings Strategies

## Emergency Fund Essentials
- Purpose: A cash buffer designed solely for unexpected crises (sudden medical bills, car repairs, job loss). It prevents you from turning to high-interest credit card debt.
- Target Amount: 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses (rent, groceries, utilities, minimum debt payments).
- Storage: Keep emergency funds in a High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA) earning competitive APY (typically 4% to 5%), where the funds are liquid and FDIC-insured, rather than in the stock market.

## Debt Payoff Strategies
1. Debt Avalanche Method:
   - Make minimum payments on all debts.
   - Put all extra money toward the debt with the HIGHEST interest rate (e.g., credit card debt at 24% APR).
   - Mathematically saves the most money in interest over time.
2. Debt Snowball Method:
   - Make minimum payments on all debts.
   - Put all extra money toward the debt with the SMALLEST balance, regardless of interest rate.
   - Provides quick psychological wins and momentum to stay motivated.

## Credit Scores and Healthy Utilization
- Credit Utilization Ratio: The percentage of your available revolving credit line you are currently using. Aim to keep this below 30%, and ideally under 10%.
- Payment History: Always pay your statement balance in full before the due date every month to avoid paying any interest and to build an excellent credit score (> 750).
